Reading these numbers

The three matches are not equally observed, so the boards show the evidence behind every figure instead of a single clean ranking.

  • Smoky Mountain Forge has a video-reviewed scoring timeline and 81% of its plays attributed to a team — the only match where scoring comparisons are solid.
  • Highlander Summit attributes 35% of plays by robot proximity, with no review pass.
  • Mall of America attributes 1 play out of 22. Its camera sits low and a referee blocks the near side, so robots have a position for only 40–66% of the match. Its teams appear in the field-use board and are marked not assessed everywhere scoring is involved.
  • Highlander's tracker coasts through occlusion instead of dropping a robot, and its output cannot distinguish a detection from a prediction — so its 100% position coverage is marked coasted, not detected rather than treated as fully observed.
  • A team marked not assessed was not measured at this depth. It is not a zero.
