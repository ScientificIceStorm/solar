Train the robot detector

Save at least eight reviewed frames, six with visible robots, spread across the match. Frames where the model missed or mis-boxed a robot teach it the most. Training runs on your GPU in this browser, holds back every fourth frame (or your last video) to check itself, and is only saved if it beats the base model on those frames.

Teach scoring

Uses the frame loaded above. Mark the four inside floor corners clockwise from top-left, check the numbered goal boxes, then enter the pins on each goal and who owns each toggle. Tick Can't see for anything blocked. Label frames from different moments, including before and after stacks change. Three frames is the minimum; ten or more is better.

Check the scoring

Seven quick checks per match. Each shows a couple of goals and all four toggles at one moment, filled in with what the replay read. Fix any pin count or toggle that's wrong, or tick Can't see if it's blocked. Your answers teach ScoutVision this camera, then you can rebuild the replay with them.
