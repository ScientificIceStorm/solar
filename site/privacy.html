Privacy policy

Effective November 27, 2026

Solar (skyloft.tech) is a free site for VEX V5 Robotics Competition rankings, event pages, team scouting and ScoutVision match analysis. This page explains what information Solar handles, why, and the choices you have. Solar is independent and is not affiliated with VEX Robotics, the REC Foundation or RobotEvents.

Competition data

Most of Solar is built from public competition records published on RobotEvents: team numbers and names, schools or organizations, locations, match results, rankings, skills scores and awards. Solar shows and analyses these records (for example ratings and win probabilities). If something about your team is wrong, the fix has to be made on RobotEvents; Solar picks it up from there.

Accounts

You can use Solar without an account. If you create one, Solar stores your email address, a securely hashed password (or your Google sign-in, if you choose “Continue with Google”) and the team number you choose to follow. Accounts are run by our authentication provider, Supabase. Your email is used only to sign you in, confirm your account and reset your password; Solar does not send marketing email and does not sell or share it. You can sign out at any time and ask us to delete your account (see Contact).

Newsletter

If you sign up for updates on the Vision UI or ScoutVision pages, Solar stores your email address, when you signed up and which page you used. It is used only to send occasional news about Solar, ScoutVision and Vision UI, and is never sold or shared. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any update or emailing us (see Contact), and your address is then deleted.

ScoutVision Match studio

  • Your video stays on your device. Videos you open in the Match studio are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to Solar.
  • Published analyses are public. When an analysis finishes, Solar publishes it unless you untick Publish to Solar: the robot paths, goal and toggle states, scoring plays, the event, match and team numbers, and the scores. Anyone can view published analyses from event and team pages. They contain no video and no account details.
  • Optional sharing. If you turn on sharing, the single frames you check or review are sent with your answers to help train ScoutVision. Sharing is off unless you turn it on.
  • Saved on your device. Replays you build, trained detector settings and scoring checks are kept in your browser’s storage until you remove them or clear your browser data.

Match videos

Event and team pages can play official event broadcasts from YouTube at the moment a match happened. Those players are provided by YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play; YouTube’s own privacy policy applies to them.

Event map (Mapbox)

The Map page uses Mapbox to draw the map. Nothing is requested from Mapbox until you open that page. When you do:

  • Your browser connects to Mapbox. It downloads the map software and map images, so Mapbox receives your IP address, browser details, the address of this site and the map areas you view.
  • Mapbox collects usage information. Its software reports events such as the map loading and how quickly it loaded, with a random identifier.
  • Data is saved in your browser. Mapbox keeps that identifier and related timestamps in local storage, and keeps downloaded map images in a cache. Clearing this site’s browser data removes them.

Solar does not send your account details or team number to Mapbox, and does not ask for your device’s location. Mapbox handles this information under the Mapbox Privacy Policy. If you prefer not to share it, don’t open the Map page.

Hosting, security and analytics

Solar is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. To limit uploads, Solar counts requests per visitor for one hour using a one-way hash; the IP address itself is not stored. Solar uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without tracking you across sites. Solar does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Children

Many VEX competitors are students. You can browse Solar without giving any personal information. Accounts are meant for people 13 and older; younger students should use Solar without an account or with a parent or teacher. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Browse without an account, and untick Publish to Solar for any analysis you want to keep private.
  • Ask us to delete your account, or to remove an analysis you published.
  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time.
  • Clear ScoutVision data saved in your browser at any time from your browser settings.

Changes

If Solar starts handling information in a new way, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
