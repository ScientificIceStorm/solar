Vision UI

A revolutionary new interface for the VEX V5 brain. Pick a route, watch it run on the field map and step through it move by move, built for VEX Override.

Autonomous, stats and PID: everything a match needs, one tap from the brain's home screen.

Watch the route run.

The brain runs Near side 2, all 13 steps, one move at a time.

    Know your robot before it breaks.

    The Stats tab checks every motor, sensor and battery.

    Tune it between runs.

    The PID tab drives a test move, plots it against the target and scores the run. Change kP, kI or kD on the screen and go again, without re-uploading.

    1. Pick drive or turn
    2. Set kP, kI and kD
    3. Run the test move
    4. Read overshoot, settle time and error

    Made for the pits, not a laptop.

    Route picker
    Every saved autonomous route as a button. The brain shows which one is armed and when it's ready to run.
    Live field map
    The route drawn on the Override field, numbered waypoint by waypoint, with forward, reverse and turn moves in their own colours.
    Step through it
    Pause, go back a step or jump ahead to test one move at a time, with the step's target, position and speed on screen.
    Stats
    Drive and lift motor heat, current and speed, odometry, IMU and battery, with a red badge on any tab that needs a look.
    PID tuning
    Tune the drive and turn controllers from the screen between runs, without re-uploading code.
